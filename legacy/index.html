School that fits your child's stamina

For case managers, therapists and families.

For medically fragile and wheelchair-dependent children, public school is an exhausting all-or-nothing trap, and summer break causes devastating physical and cognitive regression. TALA changes the paradigm with 50-week, year-round, seamless dual enrollment flexibility. Families book focused 2-hour high-support blocks or virtual sessions, matched to their child's stamina, supported by a licensed RN and a 2:1 in-person staffing ratio. We take off only recognized federal holidays. 100% funded by Arizona high-tier ESA allocations.

Questions we hear most

What documents do I need to apply?

Phase 1 asks for three things:

  • Your child's IEP
  • Nursing documentation
  • Therapy documentation

In phase 2 we'll also talk through daily activities (ADL) and transfer goals. Use the checklist to get ready.

What would my schedule look like?

You book focused 2-hour blocks, in person or online, matched to your child's stamina. We're open 50 weeks a year and close only for recognized federal holidays.
